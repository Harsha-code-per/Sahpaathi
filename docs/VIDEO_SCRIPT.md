# 3-minute video script

The submitted video is under 3:00. The voice-over is synthetic (an Indian English neural voice), and the footage is the live site, filmed by a script. The airplane-mode scenes ran with the network switched off. Words follow the education mentor's review: a companion, engagement rather than learning.

**Problem.** In over a lakh Indian schools, one teacher handles every grade at once. While she works with one group, the others wait, unguided. And many of those children speak a language at home that isn't the language of the classroom.

**Solution.** Sahpaathi is a classroom companion, in a five-hundred-rupee speaker. While the teacher sits with the children who need her most, it keeps another group engaged with a structured audio activity, in their home language, with no internet. It supports the teacher; it doesn't replace her.

**Studio.** It starts in the Studio. Claude writes each lesson from a textbook chapter and a district: local names, crops, free props like pebbles. Then, four checks. Code recomputes every sum. A second AI scores safety and accuracy, and translates every line back to English. A native speaker corrects and approves. In our test, code and AI flagged all twenty planted mistakes, and pinpointed eighteen. Only approved lessons get a voice.

**Teacher.** In class, the phone stays in airplane mode. The teacher sees today's plan in her own language, how to set up the room, and what to keep ready. One tap to start.

**Branch.** The speaker leads the children, then waits for them to answer. After each part, she glances up and taps: most, some, or few got it. The lesson moves to stretch, more practice, or easier, on the phone. No tap? It carries on after ten seconds.

**Calls.** After class, she marks who was absent. When the phone finds a signal, each absent child is queued for a catch-up call to a parent's basic phone: a recap, then press one to repeat, two if understood, three for help. This is our simulator; live calling is ready to connect.

**Admin.** Block officers see how lessons land: which classes need support, and which parts were hardest. Sample data is always labelled.

**Tutor.** For parents at home, there's also a prototype helper. It explains any topic or textbook page, asks questions, and explains again.

**Close.** No login for the teacher. Nothing to prepare. One tap to start, one tap to adapt. After an education mentor's review, we claim engagement, not learning, until we've tested with real teachers and children. That's next.
