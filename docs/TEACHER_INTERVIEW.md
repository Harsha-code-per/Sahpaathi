# Teacher session kit

For every session with a teacher (first one: 21–22 Oct). About 45 minutes. One person leads the conversation and one takes notes, and they are never the same person. Afterwards, every finding goes into [TESTING_LOG.md](TESTING_LOG.md) within 24 hours. The judges score "proof of testing and refinement based on user input", so the log is the deliverable, not the conversation.

## What we want to learn
Each question below exists to test one of these. Write the number next to each note.

| # | We believe… | It's true if… |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | A teacher can start a lesson with no help | Lesson playing within 60 s of getting the phone, no hints |
| H2 | The checkpoint tap fits into teaching another grade | Taps within the 10 s, without stopping the other group for long |
| H3 | Children can follow the guide over a ₹500 speaker | Teacher says the pace, voice and volume are right for Grade 1–3 |
| H4 | The episode is correct and feels local | No wrong fact, word or name the teacher would change |
| H5 | Hindi-speaking children can follow the bridge to Tamil | Teacher thinks the migrant children in their class could follow it |
| H6 | Attendance after the lesson takes under a minute | Absent rolls saved in under 60 s |
| H7 | Parents would answer a catch-up call on a keypad phone | Teacher believes most families would pick up and press a key |
| H8 | A teacher would use this every week | Teacher names the day and grade they would use it for |

## Before you go
- [ ] Two printed copies of [CONSENT_FORM.md](CONSENT_FORM.md) (one for them, one for us)
- [ ] One printed observation sheet (below) per teacher
- [ ] Phone charged, the app installed from the live link, and **airplane mode on before you arrive**
- [ ] At least one Tamil-medium and one Hindi→Tamil lesson imported, plus the built-in sample
- [ ] Speaker charged and paired, volume set once, at 3 m distance
- [ ] A stopwatch; pen and paper (no laptop on the teacher's desk)
- [ ] Headteacher's permission if children will be in the room. **Do not photograph or record children at all.**

## Script
**1. Consent (2 min).** Read the form aloud in Tamil, answer questions, and both sign. If they say no to any part, cross it out and carry on.

**2. Their classroom (8 min).** Ask, then listen. Don't mention Sahpaathi yet.
- Which grades do you teach together, and how many children in each?
- When you teach one grade, what are the others doing? What goes wrong?
- Which languages do the children speak at home? What happens when a child doesn't understand Tamil yet? (H5)
- How often are children absent? What happens to the lessons they miss? (H7)
- What phone do you have? Does the school have a speaker, electricity and mobile signal? (H8)

**3. Hands-on (15–20 min).** Give the phone and say only: *"Please play the Grade 1 lesson to the children, as if your Grade 3 needs you."* Then stay quiet.
- Don't explain the screen. If they're stuck for 30 s, give the smallest hint and write down exactly what you said.
- Ask them to think aloud: *"Tell me what you're looking for."*
- Let at least one checkpoint come up. If they don't tap, let the 10 s fallback run and ask afterwards what they expected.
- Ask them to press **Need a simpler analogy** once.
- At the end, ask them to mark two children absent and save attendance.

**4. Content (5 min).** Show one segment's script.
- *"Is anything here wrong, odd or not how children speak here?"*
- *"Which names, foods or games would you change?"* (H4)

**5. Debrief (8 min).**
- What was confusing? What would you remove?
- Was the voice too fast, too slow, too loud or too soft for your children? (H3)
- Which grade and which day of the week would you use this? (H8)
- If a parent got a call that played the missed lesson, would they pick up? Would they press 1, 2 or 3? (H7)
- What would stop you using it next week?

**6. Close.** Thank them, leave their copy of the consent form, and ask whether you may come back with the improved version.

## Rules for the note-taker
- Write what they **do and say**, not what you think it means. Quote them word for word (in Tamil is fine).
- Use a role, never a name: "T1, Grade 1–3 teacher, Tamil-medium govt school, Madurai".
- Time every task on the sheet. A failure is a finding, not a problem: don't help to make it pass.

## After the session (within 24 hours)
1. One row in [TESTING_LOG.md](TESTING_LOG.md) per finding, not per session.
2. Mark each finding *fix before the next session*, *fix before submission* or *roadmap*, and assign an owner.
3. When a fix merges, put its PR link in the row. That observed → changed → PR chain is what the deck's testing pages show.
4. Scan the signed consent form and any photos to the team drive, never to git, and link them from the log.
